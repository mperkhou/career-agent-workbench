## Operating Rules

- Follow root `AGENTS.md` before editing, validating, committing, pushing, or
  closing out release work.
- Treat P steps as bounded implementation phases and G steps as functional
  reassessment gates. Use exactly `P01/G01`, `P02/G02`, and `P03/G03` for the
  approved scope unless a gate authorizes a narrowly scoped `Pnn-Rn` repair.
- Record `begin` before a dispatched P step mutates files. Record sanitized
  evidence before marking a P step complete or assessing its G gate.
- Preserve normal reviewed commits. Repairs append commits; do not amend,
  rebase, squash, reorder, reset, or force-push reviewed history.
- The planner may amend only incomplete/future work. An amendment updates this
  plan and the same-version changelog entry together, is committed normally,
  and is followed by an explicit tracker rebind.
- Do not push, open or mutate a PR, merge, tag, publish, deploy, contact an
  external party, retire the legacy fallback, or perform operational cutover
  without fresh explicit user authority.
- Do not make provider/model calls, authenticated LinkedIn requests,
  application submissions, employer contact, or private application-state
  mutations as part of validation.
- Keep real profile data, identifiers, dotenvs, databases, artifacts, raw
  logs, screenshots, and content-bearing evidence out of Git. Small sanitized
  control evidence may live beneath the ignored workflow runtime directory;
  heavy or sensitive evidence belongs under a disposable system temporary
  directory.
- Evidence routes are read-only. They may inspect only the declared code,
  synthetic fixtures, disposable validation results, or content-free
  aggregates and may recommend `continue`, `amend`, or `pause`; they cannot
  authorize changes.
- Gates assess the authorized functional behavior. They do not broaden into a
  generalized security review, architecture redesign, legacy monolith
  restoration, pixel-parity exercise, deployment project, or unrelated UI
  rewrite.

## Legacy/Public Chromium Comparison Protocol

Each P step produces one bounded side-by-side Chromium result for its approved
capabilities. Each matching G gate requires a read-only evidence route that
audits the sanitized result and may repeat read-only navigation. Mutating
browser operations belong to P-step validation and never to a controller
evidence route.

- Create two fresh disposable workspaces from the same accepted ops-dev
  snapshot: one for the frozen legacy application and one for the public
  application. Never share a database between the two processes.
- Verify the legacy commit, tree, and authorized-diff digest before launch and
  again after cleanup. Any drift pauses the workflow.
- Launch the legacy and public Flask applications on separate dynamically
  assigned loopback ports with explicit disposable database and output paths.
  Use Playwright Chromium from the public `.venv` to control both applications.
- Start and stop only exact owned child process IDs. Do not use the legacy
  Makefile's port-scanning stop behavior. Disable repository-local bytecode and
  cache writes, and place logs, browser homes, screenshots, PDFs, databases,
  downloads, and other runtime material beneath the workflow's external
  disposable evidence root.
- Use pre-seeded rows, deterministic fixtures, or bounded offline/stubbed
  executors wherever an interaction would otherwise call a provider or model.
  Make no authenticated LinkedIn request, application submission, employer
  contact, or other external-state mutation.
- Compare observable outcomes for the approved capability IDs: available
  controls, target behavior, stage/state transitions, selection/lineage,
  extracted text, page count, and bounded layout geometry. Do not require
  identical implementation structure, obsolete mechanics, or pixel parity.
  Safer or explicitly improved public behavior is not a regression merely
  because it differs from legacy behavior.
- Keep only sanitized counts, booleans, state names, page counts, control
  counts, content digests, and pass/fail findings in repository-local workflow
  evidence. Store raw screenshots, PDFs, logs, and content-bearing artifacts
  outside the repository and delete the disposable comparison workspaces after
  evidence is finalized.
- Verify both child processes are stopped, both disposable copies are removed,
  persistent ops-dev bindings are unchanged, and neither repository worktree
  gained a new change before a P step can complete or a G gate can pass.

## Global Pause Conditions

- The tracker binding, plan digest, revision, branch, bootstrap commit, or
  entrance baseline does not match.
- Remaining work no longer matches the nine approved capability IDs or needs a
  path outside the current P-step boundary.
- Validation failure requires a broader refactor, migration, security phase,
  provider change, or architecture project.
- A step risks live tracker data, generated artifacts, credentials, private
  content, retained full-ops state, or the frozen legacy/PR #1 evidence.
- A requested action would push, open or mutate a PR, merge, tag, publish,
  deploy, contact a third party, cut over operations, or retire the fallback
  without explicit current authority.
- Required evidence is missing, private, content-bearing, contradictory, or
  cannot be obtained from a disposable or read-only route.
- The frozen legacy commit, tree, authorized-diff digest, launch surface, or
  cleanup invariant differs from its binding, or a bounded comparison cannot
  run without writing to the legacy checkout or using live/private state.

## Completion Definition

This implementation workflow is technically complete only when G03 has
accepted all nine capabilities and all release-readiness evidence against the
exact branch history. Technical completion does not itself authorize release
or cutover. The legacy application remains the operational fallback until a
separate explicit cutover decision, and the workflow remains paused at every
external-state or release boundary.
